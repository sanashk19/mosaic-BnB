# Code Of Conduct

UQUVLI contributors are expected to keep discussions respectful, practical and focused on inclusive education.

## Expected Behavior

- Be clear and constructive.
- Assume accessibility and privacy concerns are product requirements, not optional polish.
- Respect people with disabilities, families, teachers and researchers.
- Avoid language that shames learners or caregivers.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, insults or discriminatory language.
- Publishing private student, family, school or research data.
- Pressuring contributors to disclose personal or medical information.
- Dismissing accessibility concerns without technical or pedagogical reasoning.

Maintainers may edit, hide or remove comments and contributions that violate these expectations.
